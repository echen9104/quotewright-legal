Quotewright Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Quotewright ("the app") helps contractors calculate materials and build quotes. This policy explains what information the app handles, in plain language.

The short version: Quotewright has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The quotes, prices and customer details you enter stay on your device. We don't receive them. The only outside service in the app is the one that processes the optional Pro purchase.

Who we are

Quotewright is published by Eric Chen ("we", "us"). Contact: support@quotewright.app

What the app stores on your device

When you use the app, it saves the following on your device only:

  • Your settings: unit system (imperial or metric), currency, language, and how long your quotes stay valid.
  • Your business details: the name, address, phone number, email and license number of each business you quote under.
  • Your price book, "My items" and templates: the material and labor prices, saved line items and job templates you enter or save.
  • Your saved customers: the name, phone number, email and address of customers you add or choose to save when you export a quote.
  • Your quotes and quote history: line items, quantities, prices, markup and tax, notes, and any

customer name, phone, email, billing address and job address you type in.

  • Your Pro status: whether your Pro subscription is active.

This information is kept in the app's local storage. We do not have access to it, and it is not sent to our servers (the app has none). The app only reads your device's language and region setting to choose a default language, units and currency.

Customer details are yours. If you type a customer's name, phone number, email or address into a quote, or save a customer, that information stays on your device and appears only in the quotes and PDFs you create. You decide what to enter, and you are responsible for handling your customers' information appropriately.

PDFs and sharing

When you export a quote, the app creates a PDF on your device and opens your device's share or print screen. The same is true when you export your quote history as a CSV file for your accounting. Where the file goes after that (email, messages, a printer, cloud storage) is up to you and the app you choose. We don't see or receive it.

Purchases

Quotewright offers an optional "Pro" subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your card or payment details.

To confirm your subscription and to restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that verifies purchases. RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and entitlement status, and basic technical information such as your device platform, app version and IP address. This is used only to confirm that your subscription is valid. RevenueCat's handling of that information is described in its own privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or ask you to sign in.
  • We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or share your information for marketing.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

The web version

If you use Quotewright in a web browser, your settings, prices and quotes are saved in your browser's local storage on your computer. Clearing your browser data removes them. The web version cannot make purchases.

Backups

Your device's own backup service (such as iCloud or Google backup) may include the app's data as part of a device backup. That is controlled by your device settings and the backup provider's policies, not by us.

Your choices and deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, you control it:

  • Delete a saved quote from the app's history, or use "Clear quote" to start a new one.
  • Remove saved items from "My items".
  • Delete the app to remove all of its stored data from your device. In a web browser, clear the

site's data.

You can ask us about purchase information by contacting us at the email above. We may need to refer you to RevenueCat or to Apple or Google, which hold purchase records.

Children

Quotewright is a tool for working contractors and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data stays on your device, protected by your device's own security (such as a passcode). Purchase verification is sent over encrypted connections. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please keep your device up to date and protected.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. We do not keep your quotes, prices or customer details on any server, so there is nothing of that kind for us to access or delete beyond what is on your own device. For the limited purchase information described above, contact us and we will help, or direct you to the right provider.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.

Contact
